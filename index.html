Overview

3D plant organ segmentation must generalize across species, growth stages, and highly heterogeneous plant structures, from sparse young seedlings to dense, self-occluding canopies. A single fixed spatial scale rarely suits all of these cases at once. We study adaptive granularity selection, choosing the operating scale per plant or per region rather than fixing it in advance, and show its effect on segmenting points into organs such as stem and leaf.
